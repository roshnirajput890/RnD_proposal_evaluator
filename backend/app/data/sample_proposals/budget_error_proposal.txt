AI-POWERED MENTAL HEALTH CHATBOT FOR COLLEGE STUDENTS

Problem Statement:
College students face unprecedented mental health challenges, with 60% reporting anxiety or depression (American College Health Association, 2023). University counseling centers have 3-6 week wait times. Students need immediate, accessible support between appointments and after hours.

Proposed Solution:
We will develop an AI chatbot trained on evidence-based cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) techniques to provide 24/7 mental health support to college students. The chatbot will screen for crisis situations, provide coping strategies, connect students to campus resources, and escalate severe cases to human counselors.

Technical Approach:
Our system will use GPT-4 fine-tuned on a curated dataset of:
- 10,000+ therapist-patient conversation transcripts (anonymized, IRB-approved)
- CBT therapy manuals and evidence-based intervention protocols
- Crisis intervention guidelines from SAMHSA and JED Foundation

Key features:
- Natural language processing for empathetic, context-aware responses
- Sentiment analysis to detect crisis indicators (suicidal ideation, self-harm mentions)
- Integration with campus counseling center scheduling systems
- HIPAA-compliant data storage and encryption

We will pilot with 5 universities (15,000 students) over 12 months, measuring anxiety/depression scores using validated PHQ-9 and GAD-7 assessments.
