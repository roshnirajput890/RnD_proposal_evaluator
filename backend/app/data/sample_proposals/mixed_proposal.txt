BLOCKCHAIN-BASED SUPPLY CHAIN TRANSPARENCY PLATFORM

Problem Statement:
Supply chain opacity enables counterfeit products, unethical labor practices, and environmental violations. Consumers lack visibility into product origins and manufacturing conditions. Current tracking systems are fragmented across proprietary databases that don't communicate. We estimate $600B in annual losses from counterfeit goods globally.

Proposed Solution:
We propose a blockchain-based platform that creates immutable records of product journey from raw materials to consumer. Each participant (farmer, manufacturer, distributor, retailer) adds verified data to a shared ledger. Smart contracts automatically flag anomalies and verify compliance with ethical standards.

Technical Approach:
Our platform will use Ethereum blockchain for transparency and decentralization. We will develop:
- Mobile apps for supply chain participants to scan QR codes and upload data
- Smart contracts to verify compliance with labor and environmental standards
- Consumer-facing web portal to view product history
- Integration APIs for existing ERP systems

The system leverages blockchain's inherent security features. Data cannot be altered once recorded. We will pilot with 3 companies in the fashion industry.
