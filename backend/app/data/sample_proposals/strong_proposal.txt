CRISPR-BASED EARLY DETECTION SYSTEM FOR VIRAL PANDEMICS

Problem Statement:
Current viral detection methods require 24-48 hours for results and centralized laboratory infrastructure, delaying outbreak response and enabling rapid spread. The COVID-19 pandemic demonstrated how detection delays cost lives and economies. We need point-of-care diagnostics that deliver results in under 30 minutes with lab-quality accuracy.

Proposed Solution:
We will develop a handheld CRISPR-Cas13 biosensor that detects viral RNA in saliva samples within 20 minutes, achieving 95%+ sensitivity and specificity. Our proprietary multiplexed CRISPR guide design enables simultaneous detection of 10+ viral targets, making the device pandemic-ready for both known and emerging threats.

Technical Approach:
Building on our Nature Biotechnology 2023 publication demonstrating CRISPR-based detection of SARS-CoV-2 variants, we will:
1. Engineer multiplexed CRISPR guide libraries targeting conserved viral sequences
2. Develop a microfluidic chip for sample processing and fluorescence detection
3. Create smartphone-integrated readout with cloud-based result tracking
4. Validate against 500+ clinical samples across 5 hospital partners

Our team combines:
- Dr. Sarah Chen (PI): 15+ years CRISPR diagnostics, 40 peer-reviewed papers
- Dr. James Park: Microfluidics expert, 3 FDA-cleared devices
- Dr. Maria Rodriguez: Clinical validation specialist, Johns Hopkins partnership

The device uses proven Cas13 enzymatic detection (TRL 6), integrated with our patented multiplexing platform. Manufacturing leverages existing microfluidics supply chains, enabling rapid scale-up.
